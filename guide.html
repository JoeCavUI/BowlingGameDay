How to use Bowling Tool

What this app does: you tap in what happened on each shot. Coach watches for patterns you might miss in the moment, like the lane drying up, the ball going long, or repeat 10-pins. Then it tells you what to move and which ball in your bag fits best right now.

Setup takes about 10 minutes, once. After that, logging a shot takes about 5 seconds.

2Enter your bowler info

Tap Bowler in the top bar.

  1. Name and Handedness. Handedness matters because Coach says "move left" or "move right" based on it.
  2. Ball speed at foul line (mph) and Rev rate (rpm). Your best guess is fine. Dominance fills in automatically.

🎳 Your Barnes chart

Chris Barnes sorts bowling balls into six boxes: Strong, Medium or Weak, each Smooth or Sharp. ZVL Bowling adds a seventh row for Urethane. Inside each row, balls go from Early (reads the lane sooner) to Late. The Arsenal page draws this chart for your balls, and the Early → Late position is worked out for your speed, revs, tilt and rotation, so a speed player's chart looks different from a cranker's.

  • Coverage: "6 of 6 boxes covered" means at least one active ball in every box. The Bowler page shows the same number.
  • Gaps, steps and ideas under the chart: empty boxes (with what to look for), crowded boxes, near-duplicates, big steps in hook, and whole boxes that read early or late for you.
  • 💡 Resurface before you buy: if sanding or polishing a ball you own would fill an empty box (without opening a new hole), the app says so. A resurface costs far less than a new ball.
  • Tap a ball to tune it. 📈 On your Barnes chart shows where it sits now and where a different surface would move it.
  • 🛒 Before you buy: in + Add a ball, enter the specs and tap Preview review. The app tells you whether it fills an empty box, duplicates a ball you own, or fills a big step. Tap Add what-if ball to see it on the chart as a dashed circle.
  • Retire a ball: tap it, then Retire this ball. It leaves your chart, bags and Coach (history and Scores stay). If it was your only ball in a box, the app tells you what to look for to replace it. Tick show retired to see them, and tap one to bring it back.

🩺 Arsenal check (top of the Arsenal page): Coach compares your equipment to your game. It checks ball weight vs. your speed at the pins, signs you're tiring late in the night, speed- or rev-dominance vs. your covers, RG vs. your rev rate, tilt, and how narrow your bag is for what you rated on LASER. It also flags crowded categories, near-duplicate balls, gaps in your step-down, surfaces that are all the same, a missing spare ball, a bag that doesn't fit the oil you bowl on, balls you never throw, a ball that underperforms at a house, and resurfacing that's due. Pick All balls, Only balls I added, or one of your bags at the top.

Weight & care: tap a ball in the Arsenal Matrix to set its weight (default 15 lb) and tap Just resurfaced after a resurface. Coach counts games on each ball from Game Day and tells you when resurfacing is due (usually every 60–100 games).

Tuning: tap any ball in the Arsenal Matrix to open its sliders. If a ball hooks more or less for you than the numbers suggest, nudge Hook, Length or Backend. If you've resurfaced it, change Surface. Coach uses your adjusted version.

Fix a ball: tap it on the Arsenal page to open Ball details, then ✏️ Edit. Change the name, specs or layout (pin-to-PAP, PSA-to-PAP, pin buffer; drill and VAL angles) and tap Save changes. Renaming keeps the ball's sliders, retired status, bags, Game Day shots and PinPal matches. Want to see two or three balls side by side? Tap ⚖️ on each (in the list or in Ball details), then Compare at the bottom. Entered a ball twice by mistake? Open it and tap 🗑 Delete: one copy goes, the other stays. For a ball you no longer throw, use Retire instead.

5Pack your bag

Arsenal = every ball you own. Bag = the balls you're bringing tonight. Game Day and Coach only use the balls in your bag. There are two ways to pack one.

Option A: Let Coach pick your bag (best: uses your history)

  1. Tap Plan. Enter the house, lanes, league, oil and how many balls, then Build.
  2. The 🎒 bag section gives each ball a job: Fresh (game 1, start with), Transition (game 2) and Burn (game 3), from your own results at that house. When no ball has beaten your average for a game there, Coach picks for motion and says so.
  3. Tap 🎒 Save this bag & go to Game Day. Game Day opens set to that house, lanes, league, oil and bag.
  4. The same pick is in Arsenal → Role Planner (Save & go to Game Day works there too).

Option B: Pick the balls yourself

Tap Arsenal → 🧺 Create your own bag.

  1. ① Tap the balls you're bringing. Each one shows ✓ In bag; tap again to take it out. Balls you added yourself are at the top, marked mine.
  2. ② Name it and save (e.g., "Tuesday League Bag"). It becomes tonight's bag on Game Day automatically.
  3. Optional: open Check this bag to see gaps, overlaps and which roles are covered.

To change a bag later, tap Edit under Your bags, change the balls, and save with the same name.

Bag at least 2 balls. Coach can only recommend a ball change if there's another ball in the set to switch to. A set of 3–4 balls with different strengths works best.

7Logging each shot (4 taps)

After every first ball, answer the three numbered questions in the order you see the shot, then tap Log:

  1. Your mark: did the ball go over your target?
    • Hit my mark: it went over your target with your normal speed and release.
    • Right of mark, recovered / Left of mark, held: you missed your mark, but the lane still got it back to the pocket.
    • Right of mark / Left of mark: you missed your mark and the lane didn't save it.
    • Didn't see: only if you really didn't.
  2. Pocket: Flush, Light, High, Left of pocket, Right of pocket, Brooklyn.
  3. The rule: hit your mark with your normal speed and release, and wherever the ball ends up is the lane talking (Coach may move you). Missed your mark, and it's you (Coach says repeat the shot), even if you tapped Good execution. Good shot that still went left? Tap Hit my mark + High or Left of pocket (+ Early): that's early friction.
  4. Pins left: tap Brooklyn, Flat 10 or Ringing 10, or tap the pins still standing. For a strike you can tap nothing (or tap Strike).

Then the big Log this shot button. The screen jumps back up to Coach so you can read the next call.

6-pin pop-up (4-pin for lefties): hit the pocket and leave just the 10 (7 for lefties), and Game Day asks what the 6 did: laid down weak, hit the kickback and missed, or wrapped around it. One tap.

▸ More reads (optional, folded under the pins). Open it once and it stays open:

  • Execution: Good / OK / Poor.
  • Ball reaction (only if you saw it): Early, Long, Over/under, Carrydown.
  • Ball exit off the deck on pocket hits: Straight (finished), Right or Left. For righties, exit right = deflected/weak and exit left = drove through too hard (lefties: the opposite).
  • Speed vs normal and Notes.
Minimum per shot: mark, pocket, pins, Log. That's all Coach needs to read the lane. Exit off the deck is the best optional one: it powers the energy reads and your Eyeball score.
Be honest with "Didn't see." Only use it when you really didn't see the shot. Guessing feeds Coach bad information. Using it a lot lowers your Observation rating, and Coach gets more cautious.

Games and the 10th frame (automatic)

  • The top of the shot area shows Game 1 · Frame 7, so you always know where you are.
  • On the 10th frame a yellow 🔟 banner appears. Strike in the 10th → log your fill ball(s). Not a strike → the app asks Did you make the spare? One tap: Made it (log your fill ball) or Missed.
  • Then the next game starts by itself, with the same house, lanes and pattern. No button to remember. After game 3 it asks: Save the night or Bowl game 4.

Fix a shot

Tapped the wrong thing? In Current session log, tap the shot (✏️), change mark, pocket, pins, slide/target/breakpoint or ball, and Save changes, or Delete shot. Coach re-reads the lane right away.

You don't need to log spare shots. Lane alternation is automatic.

No speed display at your house (e.g., Wonderland)? Skip Speed vs normal, or tap it by feel. Coach simply leaves speed out.

8How Coach works

Coach updates after every shot. Before your first shot it gives you a starting plan. After that, the top line of the Coach box is always the one thing to do next.

🧭 The compass: right ball, right spot

No matter how many things are going on, the game comes down to two ideas: Rick Benoit's "get the friction timing right and stay out of the mud" and Moe Pinel's "put your feet in the right spot and get the right ball in your hand." Every Coach call answers two questions:

🎳 Right ball?
Does the ball read the lane when tonight's pattern needs it to? Early enough to store energy, late enough to still have it at the pins. Coach works this out for you from each ball's cover, surface, core and layout plus your speed, revs, tilt and rotation. No sliders needed.
🎯 Right spot?
Are your feet and target keeping the breakpoint outside the exit point (rule of 31: pattern length − 31)? Turning at or inside it means changing direction in the mud.

The top of the Coach box shows the answer as a scoreboard:

  • Ball ✅ · Spot ✅ → Stay. Right ball, right spot.
  • Ball ✅ · Spot ❌ → Move your feet, keep the ball. The ball fits; the lane changed where you're playing.
  • Ball ❌ · Spot ✅ → Change the ball, keep your spot. The lane is showing the same timing problem the ball has.
  • Spot ❌ (mud) → Get out of the mud first. Then judge the ball.
  • ⚠️ means the ball is part of the problem but nothing in tonight's bag fixes it, so the move is your feet. ❔ means Coach doesn't know yet.
  • Execution first → Repeat your shot. A shot you didn't throw well can't tell you anything about the ball or the spot.
Pick tonight's oil pattern and fill in each ball's surface, RG and differential. Without them Coach can still call moves, but it can't tell if it's the ball or the spot. Missing specs are flagged as a "rough estimate".

What Coach uses, in order

  1. You: your Bowler page (speed vs. revs, tilt, rotation, hand).
  2. What you're good at adjusting: your LASER ratings (5 = strength, 1 = avoid). Coach won't suggest a move you rated 1, and it leads with the ones you rated highest.
  3. The lane: your oil pattern's length, volume and ratio. Flat/sport patterns get smaller moves. Light oil means expect the lane to dry up early.
  4. Your bag: each ball's cover, surface, core and layout. Ball changes only come from tonight's bag, and only if a ball actually fixes the problem.
  5. Each shot: did you hit your mark (miss room), where it hit the pocket, what you left, and ball reaction.

The rules Coach follows

✋ Bad shot? Don't move.
Missed your mark and the lane didn't save it (Left of mark / Right of mark), or you marked Poor execution: Coach ignores that shot for the lane read and tells you to repeat.
✅ Striking? Stay.
2 strikes in your last 3 good shots means don't touch anything.
✅ Wide window: the best look
Missed outside and it recovered to the pocket, and missed inside and it held to the pocket. You have room both ways. Stay put.
🔔 Hooking early → move
2 good shots in a row high (high pocket, 4-pin, 4-9, ringing 10, Brooklyn, crossed over, "too early"). Follow the miss: feet 2 boards left, eyes 1 left (righties; lefties go right). Or a cleaner, longer ball.
🔔 Holding / going long → move
2 good shots in a row light (light pocket, flat 10, missed light, "too long"). Feet 2 boards right, eyes 1 right (righties; lefties go left). Or slow down a touch, or an earlier-reading ball.
🔔 In the pocket but not carrying
Your line is fine. This is about how the ball finishes: a ball change, speed or release, not a big move. Your eyes decide which way: ball deflecting right off the deck or the 6-pin laying down = needs more finish; ball driving left or the 6 wrapping = too strong, go smoother.
⚠️ Early warning
Even while you're striking, if the ball deflects off the deck (or the 6-pin lays down) on 2 of your last 3 pocket hits, Coach warns you before the flat 10s start.
🔔 Over/under
Some early, some long. Coach goes smoother: a small parallel move or a smoother ball.
👀 Just made a change?
Coach waits for 2 good shots before judging it, then tells you whether it worked. If the same problem continues, it gives you the next step, usually a bigger move or a different kind of change.

🔁 Moving on your own

You don't have to wait for Coach. If you feel something has to change, move and log it. Coach reviews your moves the same way it makes its own:

  • ⏱️ Timing: moving right after a shot that missed your mark, after only one good shot, or while you're striking gets a heads-up.
  • ↔️ Direction: if your last 2 good shots were hooking early and you moved out (or holding and you moved in), Coach reminds you to follow the miss. A move the right way gets a 👍.
  • Result: after 3 good shots, ✅ it worked, or ↩️ it didn't help, with what to do: move further, come back halfway if you overshot, or go back.
  • 🔁 Too many moves: 3 changes in 5 shots and Coach asks you to stay on one line for 2 good shots.

When you save the night, Scores shows Your moves, e.g. "4 made · 3 good calls · 1 right after a poor shot", plus one thing to do better next time.

One change at a time. Coach always gives you a single move, plus what to try next if 2 more good shots say the same thing.
Help Coach help you: tapping your mark (Hit my mark / recovered / held / Left of mark / Right of mark) is what lets Coach tell a bad shot from a lane change. Tap Why & what's next for the reasons, and What Coach is using to see the profile and pattern it's working from.

🎳 The Five Elements of scoring

Every shot is made of five things: direction, angle, speed, rev rate and roll. Every Coach suggestion is labeled with the element it changes, so you learn to think in elements.

🎯 Direction
Where you stand, aim and send the ball. "Feet 2 left, eyes 1 left" is a direction move. Graded by how often you hit your mark.
📐 Angle
How far the ball comes back from the breakpoint to the pocket. Coach measures it from your target, breakpoint and pocket board. Weak hits on a direct line → open your angle (feet move, eyes stay). Too strong on a big swing → tighten your angle (eyes move more than feet). Enter your breakpoint so Coach can see angle.
⚡ Speed
Faster = longer and later; slower = earlier. Tap Speed vs normal: a faster shot that went long is you, not the lane, so Coach won't move you off it.
🔄 Rev rate
From your Bowler page. Shapes how every ball in your bag rolls for you, and whether you're speed- or rev-dominant.
🌀 Roll
Your tilt and rotation, and how the ball goes through skid → hook → roll. You see it in the exit off the deck and the 6-pin: a weak exit means the ball rolled out early; driving through too hard means it was still turning hard.
  • Bowler page: the Five Elements: your profile card explains each element for you in plain words.
  • End of the night: Save Session (and Scores) shows a grade for each element and one drill for your weakest.
  • Coach also watches: lane-by-lane differences ("lane 24 is hooking, 23 isn't"), the lane changing from game to game, and your breakpoint drifting.

🌀 Energy and 🏈 the Mud

Two simple questions sit on top of all the data:

🌀 Does the ball get to the pins with energy left?
The ball leaves your hand with axis rotation and uses it up going down the lane (skid → hook → roll). Ran out (weak exit, 6-pin laying down, flat 10s): protect the energy. Stay in the oil a little longer, use a cleaner ball, or add a touch of speed. Too much left (driving through hard, 6 wrapping, ringing 10s): let it read a little sooner. Move toward the friction, use a smoother ball, or take a touch off.
🏈 Is it changing direction in the mud?
Where the oil ends and breaks down, friction is uneven. A ball asked to turn there is like a receiver cutting on a muddy field: it slips one shot and grabs the next (over/under). Get it to turn in clean friction instead.

Rule of 31: the exit point (not the breakpoint)

Pattern length − 31 ≈ the board where the ball should exit the end of the pattern. On a 42 ft pattern that's about board 11. The breakpoint is further downlane and usually a few boards further outside, in the dry. If your breakpoint is at or inside the exit board, the ball is turning in the mud. Enter your breakpoint and pick your oil pattern on Game Day so Coach can check this.

📐 Three-point line: make your line go through the exit point

Game Day checks your line with Kegel's Math for a Line, worked backwards from your slide at the foul line, through your target, to the rule of 31 exit point:

  1. Exit point (the focal point) = pattern length − 31. On 38 ft that's board 7.
  2. Target minus exit point: target 10 − 7 = 3.
  3. Divide by 3 = 1.
  4. Add to your target for the laydown board: 10 + 1 = 11.
  5. Add your laydown offset (usually 5, set on the Bowler page) for the slide board: 11 + 5 = 16.
  6. Slide 16, lay down 11, over 10, out at 7.

Under your Slide / Target boxes, Game Day checks your line in two stages:

  • Game 1, before you've moved: starting line. Does your slide and target send the ball through the rule-of-31 exit point? ✅ Starting line checks out, or a mild ⚠️ heads-up with the slide the math wants. It's a starting point for a fresh pattern, not a rule.
  • After a move, or from game 2: usable line. As the lane changes, the place the ball should leave the oil moves too. Coach moves the exit point by the same number of boards your breakpoint has moved since your first shots (breakpoint 8 → 11 means exit point 10 → 13), then checks that your slide and target still go through it: ✅ Usable line or ⚠️ Usable-line heads-up. Log your breakpoint so Coach can follow it.

Each board your slide is off moves the ball about 3 boards at the end of the oil, so small slide changes matter.

How Coach uses it: the starting plan gives you the full starting line for your target. A starting-line heads-up appears before you've made any moves. If you keep missing your mark and the line doesn't line up, Coach says to fix your slide first: the misses may be alignment, not your throw. These are heads-ups, never the main call.
Like the rule of 31, the ÷3 and +5 are rules of thumb. If your ball consistently lands more or less than 5 boards from the inside of your slide foot, change Laydown offset on the Bowler page.

📋 Game Plan (before you bowl)

Open Plan, type where you're bowling (and your lanes if you know them), and tap Build my game plan. Coach writes tonight's plan from your data:

  • Where you stand at this house: average, this season, last time here, and whether your scores drop as the night goes.
  • What the lanes are telling you: your leaves here vs everywhere else (light hits = start with more ball; high hits = start smoother).
  • Ball: first ball and backup from your record here (recent form counts), and balls to avoid opening with.
  • Starting line: your last Game Day line here, lines from your PinPal notes, and the rule of 31.
  • If / then, Spares (your most expensive one here), Lanes (your record on the pair), and two Focus cues.
  • By lane condition: what has worked for you on fresh lanes (game 1), in transition (game 2) and when the lanes burn (game 3, plus Game Day games where you tapped Early or High), by cover, core, layout, surface and ball.
  • Reading early vs weak: 10 pins and washouts can mean the ball never hooked or that it read early and burned up. When high hits rise at the same time, Coach calls it early and tells you to go smoother before moving left. Tap Early / Long in Game Day ④ so Coach knows which.
  • Your bag (pick How many balls?): Coach first checks how your covers, cores, layouts and Barnes boxes have done at this house and pair, then picks the balls: the one to start with, plus balls that read earlier (more ball) and later (for transition), each with its own job.

Save as PDF opens the print screen: tap the share icon, then Save to Files. Share text sends it to Notes or a message.

Every line comes from your games. When there isn't enough data (a new house, a pair with few games), the plan says so instead of guessing. The more nights you log in Game Day, the better it gets.

9Between games and saving the night

  • Start next game: tap it at the start of each new game. That's how Coach tracks the lane changing game to game.
  • The Lane scoreboard shows left lane vs right lane: strike %, pocket %, carry. Useful when one lane plays differently from the other.
  • When you're done, tap Save Session. This moves the night into Scores and adds an arsenal review: what your bag was missing and the best in-bag ball for the conditions you saw.
  • 👁️ Eyeball score (0–100) shows in the Coach box and when you save: how much of what matters you actually watched. That's your mark (miss room), where it hit the pocket, where the ball left the deck on pocket hits, and the key pin on corner-pin leaves. Scores shows it for every night, with your trend. Best ball is your eyeball.
  • Reset current session clears the log without saving. Only use it if you want to start over.
Your in-progress session is saved as you go. If the app closes or your phone locks mid-game, just open it again.

Troubleshooting

Found a problem? Take a screenshot, then Home → 💬 Send feedback. It fills in your app version and phone; add the screenshot and send it.

"Pick a current ball" when I tap Log

Pick a Bag in Game Day → Tonight. If the list is empty, pack one in Arsenal → Create your own bag first.

Coach never suggests a different ball

Make sure the set has 2 or more balls, and that you're tracking leaves and ball reaction. On a Blend / Typical read, Coach usually says stay where you are, and that's normal.

My ball isn't in the list

Add it on Arsenal → + Add a ball → Save to Arsenal, then add it to your bag on the Bag page.

My oil patterns, houses or bags are missing

Your data lives on the device and app where you entered it. The Home Screen app and a Safari tab keep separate data, and your laptop's data doesn't reach your phone. Add the pattern in the app you bowl with (Game Day has a quick Add button next to Oil Pattern), or move everything with Back up on one and Restore on the other.

Lane numbers look wrong

Type the pair as 23-24 (left lane first) and set Starting lane correctly.

My data is gone

Use Home → Restore from backup. Also make sure you're opening the app from the home-screen icon, not a Safari tab. They keep separate data.
