How to use Bowling Tool

What this app does: you tap in what happened on each shot. Coach watches for patterns you might miss in the moment, like the lane drying up, the ball going long, or repeat 10-pins. Then it tells you what to move and which ball in your bag fits best right now.

Setup takes about 10 minutes, once. After that, logging a shot takes about 5 seconds.

1Install it on your iPhone

  1. Open the app link in Safari (not Chrome, and not the preview inside Messages).
  2. Tap Share, the square with the up arrow.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. From now on, open it from the Bowling icon on your home screen.
Opening it from the icon makes it full-screen, lets it work with no signal at the lanes, and keeps your data safer.

2Enter your bowler info

Tap Bowler in the top bar.

  1. Name and Handedness. Handedness matters because Coach says "move left" or "move right" based on it.
  2. Ball speed at foul line (mph) and Rev rate (rpm). Your best guess is fine. Dominance fills in automatically.
  3. PAP, Axis tilt and Axis rotation if you know them (your pro shop can tell you). Leave them blank if not.
  4. LASER ratings (1–5): how comfortable you are adjusting Loft, Aim, Speed and Release. Be honest.
  5. Tap Save.
Speed, revs, tilt and rotation change how Coach thinks each ball will react for you. The LASER ratings decide which moves Coach suggests first. If you rate Speed 1, Coach won't tell you to change speed.

3Add your bowling house and oil pattern

Tap Oil.

Houses

  1. In Houses, type your bowling center (e.g., "Shelby Lanes") and tap Add.

Oil patterns

  1. Enter a Pattern Name (e.g., "House Shot" or "Shark").
  2. Fill in what you know: Length (ft), Volume (mL), Ratio, and Surface notes (e.g., "tight middle, crisp backends"). All optional.
  3. Tap Save pattern.
Game Day groups your history by house, league and pattern. Next time you bowl the same league, Game Day shows what worked last time.

4Add your balls

Tap Arsenal. A starter list of balls is already loaded. If yours are on it, skip to step 5.

  1. Tap + Add a ball.
  2. Enter Brand, Ball name, Cover and Core (required), plus Surface (e.g., 2000, 4000, Polish), RG, Diff and PSA if you have them. Specs are on the manufacturer's website.
  3. Fill in the layout section if you know your drilling.
  4. Tap Save to Arsenal.
Save to Arsenal = a ball you own. You can bag it and bowl with it.
Add what-if ball = a ball you're thinking about buying. It shows as a dashed tag for comparison only.

Tuning: tap any ball in the Arsenal Matrix to open its sliders. If a ball hooks more or less for you than the numbers suggest, nudge Hook, Length or Backend. If you've resurfaced it, change Surface. Coach uses your adjusted version.

5Build your bag (a "set")

A set (or bag) is the balls you're bringing tonight. There are two ways to make one.

Option A: Let Coach pick your bag (fastest)

  1. Tap Arsenal and scroll to Role Planner.
  2. Under How many balls in your bag?, tap 2 3 4 6 or 9. To choose only from balls you added yourself, tick Only balls I added first.
  3. Coach picks the balls that cover the most different lane conditions without doubling up.
  4. In the Use this bag on Game Day? box, uncheck anything you aren't bringing, name the bag, and tap Save & go to Game Day. The bag is already selected when Game Day opens.

Option B: Pick the balls yourself

Tap Analyze.

  1. In the Ball list, tap each ball you're bringing. Use the filter box to find one fast.
  2. Check Gaps + overlaps and Coach summary. They show if your bag is missing a role (e.g., nothing for when the lanes burn up) or has two balls that do the same thing. Tap a missing role to see suggestions.
  3. Type a Set name (e.g., "Tuesday League Bag") and tap Save current selection.
Bag at least 2 balls. Coach can only recommend a ball change if there's another ball in the set to switch to. A set of 3–4 balls with different strengths works best.

6Game Day: set up before your first shot

Tap Game Day. In 1) Environment + setup:

  1. House: pick your center.
  2. League: pick one, or tap Add next to it to create one for that house.
  3. Lanes / Pair: type your pair, e.g., 23-24.
  4. Starting lane: left or right lane of the pair. The app then alternates lanes for you every frame.
  5. Oil Pattern and Arsenal set: pick the pattern and the bag you saved.

In 2) Line + ball:

  1. Current ball: the ball you're starting with. You can also tap a ball in the Ball list panel.
  2. Feet, Target at arrows and Breakpoint as board numbers. These stay filled in between shots, so you only change them when you move.
If you see a "Before you bowl" banner, a step is missing. Tap the link in it.

7Logging each shot (about 5 seconds)

After every first ball, in 3) Quick reads:

  1. Leave / result: tap Strike, Brooklyn, Flat 10 or Ringing 10. For anything else, tap the pins left standing on the pin deck.
  2. Miss room: did the shot go where you meant it to?
    • No miss: it went where you meant.
    • Miss left held / Miss right recovered: you missed, but the lane still got it back to the pocket.
    • Miss left / Miss right: you missed and the lane didn't save it.
  3. Pocket: Flush, Light, High, or missed left or right.
  4. Ball reaction (only if you saw it): Too early, Too long, Over/under or Carrydown.
  5. Optional: Execution (Good / OK / Poor), Ball exit, Speed vs normal, Notes.
  6. If you moved or changed balls, update Feet / Arrows / Current ball.
  7. Tap Log this shot.
The three taps that matter most: leave, miss room, and pocket. Coach reads the lane from those. If you're in a hurry, do just those three and tap Log.
Be honest with "Didn't track." Only use it when you really didn't see the shot. Guessing feeds Coach bad information. Using it a lot lowers your Observation rating, and Coach gets more cautious.

Frame numbers, 10th-frame fill balls and lane alternation are automatic. You don't need to log spare shots.

8How Coach works

Coach updates by itself after every shot, starting with the 3rd. The Coach review box is right under the Log button.

What Coach looks at

  • Your last ~8 shots, mostly the ones you marked as good execution. Bad shots don't make Coach think the lane changed.
  • Leaves (10-pins, 4-pins, 2-pins, splits), miss room, pocket hits and ball reaction.
  • Each game separately, so it can spot the lane changing over the night (e.g., Game 1 typical, Game 3 burning up).

The lane reads

Blend / Typical
You're seeing: Misses still come back (held inside, recovered outside). There's room to miss.
Coach suggests: Stay put and make small moves. Wide window.
Burn
You're seeing: High hits, 4-pins, 4-9s, ringing 10s. The fronts and middle are drying up.
Coach suggests: Follow the miss: feet 2 boards left, eyes 1 board left (righties; lefties go right) to get into more oil. A touch more speed, and a cleaner, longer ball in your bag.
Carrydown
You're seeing: Flat 10s, 8s, 2-8s. The ball looks good but dies at the pins.
Coach suggests: Feet 2 boards right, eyes 1 board right (righties; lefties go left) so the ball sees friction sooner. Slightly less speed, and a stronger ball that keeps driving.
Over/Under
You're seeing: A mix of high and light hits. It hooks early one shot and goes long the next.
Coach suggests: Small, parallel moves and a smoother ball to make the lane predictable.
Flat / Tight
You're seeing: Not much miss room either way.
Coach suggests: Play tighter to the oil, use a smoother, earlier-reading ball, and don't open up the lane yet.

🔔 Coach alerts

A highlighted alert appears at the top of the Coach box when:

  • The lane read changes, e.g., "Lane read changed: Blend → Burn".
  • A pattern is confirmed and another ball in your bag fits better, e.g., "Best ball in your bag for this: Phaze 2". Coach compares your current ball to every ball in tonight's set and picks the one that best fixes the problem.
  • A problem keeps repeating, e.g., three "too long" reads.

The rest of the Coach box

  • Lane map: the read for each game, and whether it's changing.
  • You: tempo creeping faster or slower, execution drifting, or misses leaking one way.
  • LASER next move: the next 1–2 moves (Loft, Aim, Speed, Equipment, Release), picked from the ones you rated yourself good at.
  • Observation rating: how well you're tracking. Higher means Coach is more confident.
Which way to move: Coach follows the classic "2-and-1" rule: follow your miss. Ball hooking early or hitting high → move feet 2 and eyes 1 left. Ball going long or hitting light → move feet 2 and eyes 1 right. (Lefties: the opposite.)
How to use it: make one change at a time, either a move or a ball, not both. Then let Coach see 2–3 good shots before changing again. Coach is a second set of eyes. Your own read of the ball still matters most.

9Between games and saving the night

  • Start next game: tap it at the start of each new game. That's how Coach tracks the lane changing game to game.
  • The Lane scoreboard shows left lane vs right lane: strike %, pocket %, carry. Useful when one lane plays differently from the other.
  • When you're done, tap Save Session. This moves the night into Scores and adds an arsenal review: what your bag was missing and the best in-bag ball for the conditions you saw.
  • Reset current session clears the log without saving. Only use it if you want to start over.
Your in-progress session is saved as you go. If the app closes or your phone locks mid-game, just open it again.

10Scores and history

  • Tap Scores to see every saved night: house, lanes, pattern, strike rate, pocket %, execution.
  • Tap a session to see every shot and the arsenal review.
  • Logged something wrong? Tap the shot to edit or delete it.
  • Next time you set up Game Day for the same house + league + pattern, the prep box shows your best set and how each lane went last time.

11Back up your data

Everything is stored on your phone only. Back up after each league night:

  1. Go to Home and tap Back up my data.
  2. Choose Save to Files, or email or AirDrop it to yourself.

New phone, or cleared Safari? Home → Restore from backup and pick the file.

Other tools

  • Friction Timing: set how the lane is playing (head friction, response time, response length) and see which balls match.
  • Arsenal Matrix: every ball sorted Strong/Medium/Weak × Smooth/Sharp, so you can see where your bag has holes.

Troubleshooting

"Pick a current ball" when I tap Log

Pick an Arsenal set in Game Day section 1. If the set list is empty, save one on Analyze first.

Coach never suggests a different ball

Make sure the set has 2 or more balls, and that you're tracking leaves and ball reaction. On a Blend / Typical read, Coach usually says stay where you are, and that's normal.

My ball isn't in the list

Add it on Arsenal → + Add a ball → Save to Arsenal, then add it to your set on Analyze.

Lane numbers look wrong

Type the pair as 23-24 (left lane first) and set Starting lane correctly.

My data is gone

Use Home → Restore from backup. Also make sure you're opening the app from the home-screen icon, not a Safari tab. They keep separate data.
