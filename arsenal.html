Role Planner: let Coach pick my bag

Shows the best matches for each coach role from the balls currently visible on this page. Use the filter first if you want to plan from a subset.
Roles compare how each ball rolls for you against fixed motion targets. With lower revs or more speed, every ball plays later and weaker, so you'll see more Gaps here even with a full bag. The Barnes chart above shows the same thing: your balls shift to the right (later).
These roles now use a two-layer fit: your 6-box matrix family anchors what the ball naturally is, then the bowler-specific motion model checks how it rolls for you. That keeps results from overreacting to tuning alone. “Early / Control” means stronger and smoother. “Shape / Corner” means stronger with more downlane pop. A low % does not mean the ball is bad—only that it is farther from that specific role target. Covered means a strong fit, Close means usable, and Gap means the bag still lacks that motion.
