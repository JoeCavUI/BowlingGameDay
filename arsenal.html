Suggest my bag uses the same pick as your Game Plan and Game Day Coach: your record at that house and pair (weighted for sample size and recent form), your cover / core / layout patterns there, and balls spread from earlier to later. With no history for the house, it falls back to the motion roles below.
Each role's target is set inside your own range (your speed, revs and arsenal): "Early / Control" means the earliest, smoothest motion you can throw, "Burn / Friction" your cleanest and latest. The % is how close a ball comes to that job for you. “Early / Control” means stronger and smoother. “Shape / Corner” means stronger with more downlane pop. A low % does not mean the ball is bad—only that it is farther from that specific role target. Covered means a strong fit, Close means usable, and Gap means the bag still lacks that motion.
